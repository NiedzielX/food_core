# food_core

FoodCore is a groundbreaking mobile application that is revolutionizing the way we approach healthy eating and manage food allergies. Our platform offers a comprehensive dietary interview, allowing users to discover their dietary preferences and avoid cross-allergies, just as they would during a visit to a doctor.
